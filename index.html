This report has no General groups (none were shared), while the last report's total included 366 General messages from #4 and #5. Comparing country groups only, activity went up from 100 to 275, but over a third of it (108) is off-topic chat in #2 Germany.

Posted by the amber Team

  • 4 to 8 Nov: In Community #5's Canada group, the team answered Amber Scholar questions: an acceptance letter is needed to apply, and the award is paid once (not every year).
  • 3 and 10 Nov: Admin approval to join was switched off and straight back on in #4's and #5's groups.

Headline Takeaways

  • Community #5's country groups are open: it grew by 149 to 362 members, but only Canada had conversation (31 messages); the other four groups were silent.
  • Amber Scholar results date is the top question: members in #1 and #3 asked when results come out, and other members guessed "September", "late January" or "February". The team said earlier that results come by 31 January.
  • Peer explainers are helping: in #4 Canada and USA, members explained Amber Scholar and shared the official link (amberstudent.com/scholarship).
  • Useful scholarship talk in #1 UK: Commonwealth scholarships, January vs September intake funding, and the DAAD EPOS deadline (15 Nov).
  • Community #2 looks busy but isn't on topic: 108 messages in Germany, almost all chat about festivals, religion and visiting Pakistan.
  • Short.io clicks fell again: 331 in the week of 2 to 8 Nov, down 15%. /community-E (Community #5's link) is now at 594 clicks.
Needs Attention
  • Suspicious links (#3, 9 Nov): one account posted the same unknown ".xyz" links in Australia, Canada, Germany and UK. A member asked "What is this????" and there was no reply. Remove them and the account.
  • Scam and referral posts: "₦50,000 cash transfer" and "TETFUND grant" messages (#4 USA, #2 Germany), and referral sign-up links (#3 UK, #4 USA, #4 Germany).
  • Mixed Amber Scholar answers: the team told #5 an acceptance letter is required, while members in #4 told others it isn't needed and gave different results dates. One pinned FAQ (link, eligibility, acceptance letter, results date) in every community would stop the confusion.
  • Off-topic chat in #2 Germany about religion and visas; a gentle reminder of the group's purpose would help.
Data note
No General groups were shared this period, so all numbers cover the country groups only. 2 and 3 Nov fall between the last report's data (up to 2 Nov) and this report (from 4 Nov); those days had about 100 messages, mostly in #5.
